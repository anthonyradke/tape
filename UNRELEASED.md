# Unreleased

- The Alt+Z panel stays open when a Windows shell popup comes up over the game, such as the sound output picker
  (Win+Ctrl+V), quick settings or the volume flyout, so a clip keeps playing while the output device changes.
- Clips can be trimmed in the player. The cut is saved as a new clip beside the original without re-encoding, so it
  takes a moment even for a long recording and the picture is unchanged. It starts on the keyframe at or before the
  chosen point.
- The player can star, rename and delete clips. Starred clips have their own filter in the gallery, and deleted ones
  go to the Recycle Bin. Renaming isn't offered in the Alt+Z panel, where the game has the keyboard.
